**Subject:** Request: access to Al Mezan violation records

---

Dear Al Mezan,

I maintain a public, non-commercial databank of Palestine data and would like to
ask permission to include **your documented violation records** — the database
behind your published statistics.

**What we would want**: date, location, violation type, and casualty or damage
counts, with Al Mezan credited as the documenting organisation on every row.

We would not publish narrative case descriptions, and we would not present your
records as corroborated by anything other than your own documentation.

**What we would do with it**
- Store it alongside 205,118 observations from 25 other sources, each row carrying
  its origin and licence, queryable back to 1922.
- Credit you on every row the API returns, in a public ATTRIBUTION file, and on
  any page that shows your figures.
- Never re-publish your records as our own, never strip your attribution, and
  never place them in a commercial tier unless you tell us in writing that we may.
- Delete them on request, and tell you when we do.

**What we are**
An independent, non-commercial data platform for Palestine, built and run by one
person in Ramallah. It is open about its own limits: where a figure is a period
rather than a day, where a place could not be resolved, and where a source says
something we cannot verify, the API says so.

Happy to answer any question, sign anything you need signed, or take a narrower
scope than the one above if that is easier to say yes to.

With thanks and respect for your work,
