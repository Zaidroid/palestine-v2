# International Monetary Fund — Permissions

**Status:** not sent — draft for Zaid's review. `source_permission.status` moves to `asked` only when he sends.

**To:** copyright@imf.org
**Subject:** Two questions about reuse of WEO figures for the State of Palestine

---

Dear IMF Permissions team,

I maintain a public, non-commercial databank of Palestine data. We hold 474
World Economic Outlook observations for the State of Palestine (LP, NGDPD,
PPPGDP, PPPPC, BCA and related series).

I have read the Copyright and Usage terms of 11 October 2024, and I believe
the special terms in *The Use of IMF Data* already permit what we do today:
we redistribute the figures with the attribution format you specify, we do
not alter them, and we publish your terms to our own users. If that reading
is wrong, please tell me and I will change it the same day.

**Two things the terms send me to you for:**

1. **Commercial reuse.** The Data section closes with "For any potential
   commercial reuse of IMF Data, please email copyright@imf.org to request
   permission." The databank is free and non-commercial today, and we have
   accordingly marked these series as not sellable. If a commercial tier ever
   exists, may these figures be in it — and on what conditions? We are happy
   with the standalone-product disclosure your terms already describe.

2. **Automated fetching.** The general terms prohibit "the bulk download of
   information by automated technology without explicit permission", while
   the Data section permits downloading. We currently receive these series
   second-hand and would prefer to fetch them from you directly, once nightly,
   for the State of Palestine only — a few hundred values. May we, and is
   there an endpoint or rate you would prefer we use?

**How we would credit you**, on every row the API returns and in a public
attribution file:

> Source: International Monetary Fund, World Economic Outlook database,
> https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO/weo-database. © IMF.

Whatever the answer, the figures stay attributed exactly as your terms
require, and if you would rather we did not hold them at all we will remove
them and say so publicly.
