**Subject:** Permission to redistribute IODA outage detections for Palestine

---

Dear IODA team,

I maintain a public, non-commercial databank of Palestine data. We already hold
1,000 of your outage detections for Palestine, the Gaza Strip and the West Bank,
fetched from your public API, and we credit the Internet Intelligence Lab on
every one.

This letter is about redistribution rather than access. Your API returns, on
every response, `"This data is Copyright (c) 2021-2025 Georgia Tech Research
Corporation. All Rights Reserved."` We read that as written: it grants nothing,
so we have not put your rows in the downloadable data release. They remain
queryable through our API with your attribution attached, because returning a
credited fact is reporting rather than redistribution — but a bulk file is a
different act, and we would rather ask than assume.

**What we would like**: permission to include the IODA outage records we hold
for Palestine in a public, non-commercial data release — one file, under terms
you name, credited to the Internet Intelligence Lab at Georgia Tech and linking
back to ioda.inetintel.cc.gatech.edu. If a citation format or an
acknowledgement line is required, we will use exactly the one you specify.

**What we would do with it**
- Keep it alongside more than 205,000 observations from 25 other sources, each row
  carrying its origin and licence, queryable back to 1922.
- Credit you on every row the API returns, in a public ATTRIBUTION file, and on
  any page that shows your figures.
- Never re-publish your records as our own, never strip your attribution, and
  never place them in a commercial tier unless you tell us in writing that we may.
- Delete them on request, and tell you when we do.

**Why these records matter here**: an internet outage in Gaza is not a technical
footnote. It is the interval in which nothing can be reported, and having it as
a dated series next to casualty and displacement records is what lets someone
ask whether a silence in the record was a silence in the events or a silence in
the network.

**What we are**
An independent, non-commercial data platform for Palestine, built and run by one
person in Ramallah. It is open about its own limits: where a figure is a period
rather than a day, where a place could not be resolved, and where a source says
something we cannot verify, the API says so.

Happy to answer any question, sign anything you need signed, or take a narrower
scope than the one above if that is easier to say yes to.

With thanks and respect for your work,

Zaid Salem
