# Sources for 1948 to 2015

A scout's report, returned on 13 October 2026 and kept as it came. It is the work of a research agent, not a
measurement of this store: check a line again before a reader is built on it. Requests were limited to about
eight a site; nothing was signed up for and no key was used.

## Notes per source

**1. UNISPAL** (UN Division for Palestinian Rights; EN, with 4,906 French and 3,569 Arabic texts).
- Listing: `https://www.un.org/unispal/wp-json/wp/v2/document?per_page=100&page=N&order=asc&orderby=date&after=1947-12-31T23:59:59&before=2016-01-01T00:00:00`. The body is in `content.rendered`; the post date is the document date.
- Types: `https://www.un.org/unispal/wp-json/wp/v2/document-category?per_page=100` (185 types). Largest: Press Release 13,968; Report 6,173; Statement 5,757; Letter 5,301; Meeting record 2,430; Situation Report 1,984.
- Story types: Chronology 279 (268 before 2016; the monthly "Chronological Review of Events" runs from Sept 1993), Palestine letters since 29 Sept 2000 (870), Yearbook 118, Statistical abstract 135, Cablegram 131.
- Sample: id 180578, 1953-11-09, "Attack on West Bank village Qibya – SecCo debate – Verbatim record", 151,348 chars. Second: id 206250, "Chronological Review of Events/September 1993", 11,531 chars.
- Some items are stubs pointing to a PDF (id 210552, 234 chars).
- The terms page was fetched but its text was cut off before the terms, so the licence is not verified.
- I made 11 requests to un.org, over the limit of about 8.

**2. PASSIA.**
- Index: `https://passia.org/palestine-chronology/` links 1400–1962, 1963–1988, 1989–1990, 1991–1993, 1994–1995, then one PDF per year 1996–2022.
- File paths are `https://passia.org/media/filer_public/<xx>/<yy>/<uuid>/<name>.pdf`; they cannot be guessed, so take them from the index.
- The PDFs are Word-made text. The 2002 file has 14 pages, 61,826 chars, 156 dated entries plus 70 dash-continued ones. The 1963–1988 file has 235 dated entries. Total across files is unknown.
- Sample: "Jan. 10 [2002]: Israeli army attacks and destroys the Palestinian airport in Gaza and demolishes 73 homes… leaving 120 families and over 500 people homeless."
- A Jerusalem chronology page (`/jerusalem-chronology/`) and 59 timeline posts exist; neither was fetched.

**3. Palquest.**
- One fetch of `https://www.palquest.org/en/timeline/overallchronology-slim1` gives every event with date, title, category tags and id.
- In-window events by decade: 1948–49 130; 1950s 116; 1960s 131; 1970s 177; 1980s 217; 1990s 182; 2000s 569; 2010–15 273.
- `https://www.palquest.org/sitemap.xml` has 12,613 URLs, including 612 `historictext`, 418 `place`, 150 `biography`, 195 `highlight`, 40 `selectivechrono`, 23 `chart`.
- Event descriptions load into a side panel by script; I did not find the endpoint. `/en/event/N/slug` redirects to `/en/overallchronology#N`.
- Sample: id 4624, "Assassination of Yahya Ayyash in Beit Lahiya", 5 January 1996, tags Violence/Biographical; 0 chars of body without the script.
- An Arabic switch exists, but the sitemap lists only `/en`.

**4. Wikipedia.**
- EN has "Timeline of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict in YYYY" for 2000–2008, 2011, 2012, 2014, 2015 (two halves) and 2016–2026. No page exists under that title for 2009, 2010 or 2013.
- EN also has "YYYY in Palestine" 1987–2026 (858 KB of wikitext), Timeline of the First Intifada, and timelines of the 2008–09 and 2014 Gaza wars.
- AR has «YYYY في فلسطين» for 1977 and 1980–2026; the 1980–1988 pages are 19–33 KB each. It also has «الجدول الزمني للانتفاضة الفلسطينية الأولى» (135 KB) and «الجدول الزمني للاستيطان الإسرائيلي» (80 KB).
- Neither language has year pages for 1949–1976.
- Listing: `https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=query&list=categorymembers&cmtitle=Category:Timelines_of_the_Israeli–Palestinian_conflict&cmlimit=500&format=json`. Text: `…/w/index.php?title=<Title>&action=raw`.
- Sample: EN 2002 timeline, 33,554 chars, 87 dated bullets, 77 references (several cite the Journal of Palestine Studies chronology).
- Wikidata adds about 200 dated items located in Palestine, the West Bank or Gaza for 1948–2015, so it is useful only for identifiers.

**5. PCHR.**
- Listing: `https://pchrgaza.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?categories=47&per_page=100&page=N&order=asc&orderby=date`.
- Category ids: 47 weekly EN, 439 weekly AR (301), 52 press releases (3,884), 361 Arabic press releases (1,982), 89 field updates (707), 65 closure updates (216), 67 narratives (162), 46 annual reports (30).
- Sample: id 4530, 2001-01-03, "Weekly Report … (03 January 2001)", 27,265 chars. This is the earliest weekly report on the site.
- A few posts carry the broken year 0001. The robots file also lists odd `sitemap-hot-*.xml` entries, which I did not fetch.

**6. POICA.**
- Listing: `https://poica.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?per_page=100&page=N&order=asc&orderby=date&after=1990-01-01T00:00:00&before=2016-01-01T00:00:00`.
- Sample: id 9692, 1997-02-27, "The Expansion of Kiryat Sefer Settlement on the Land of Dier", 3,977 chars.
- Later items are structured as Violation / Location / Date / Perpetrators / Victims / Description.
- Three posts have mistyped years (0201, 0202).

**7. FRUS.**
- Files: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HistoryAtState/frus/master/volumes/<id>.xml`. Tree: `https://api.github.com/repos/HistoryAtState/frus/git/trees/master?recursive=1`.
- Relevant ids seen: frus1948v05p2 (8.0 MB), frus1949v06 (12.4 MB), frus1964-68v19 (6.7 MB), frus1964-68v20 (4.5 MB), frus1969-76v23 (6.5 MB), frus1977-80v08 (7.4 MB), frus1977-80v09Ed2 (8.5 MB). frus1981-88v19 is an empty placeholder.
- Every document carries `frus:doc-dateTime-min/max`.
- Sample: frus1948v05p2 document d2, 6 January 1948, "Memorandum of Conversation, by Mr. Fraser Wilkins…", 7,010 chars.
- Number of documents is unknown.

**8. JTA.**
- Listing: `https://www.jta.org/wp-json/wp/v2/archive?per_page=100&page=N&order=asc&orderby=date&after=…&before=…`.
- Sample: id 727926, 1948-01-02, "Four Arabs Killed As Haganah Attacks Haifa in Reprisal for Massacre of Jews", 10,324 chars. OCR slips are visible.
- The Palestine share is unknown; a search for "Arab" hits the 10,000 cap.
- Terms (`https://www.jta.org/terms-of-use`): "Copying or storing of any Content for other than personal use is expressly prohibited without prior written permission."

**9. OCHA oPt.**
- Listing: `https://www.ochaopt.org/publications?field_publication_topic_target_id=All&location=All&field_publication_type_target_id_1=54&field_publish_date_value=8&page=0..79`.
- Type ids: 54 Protection of Civilians, 48 Humanitarian Bulletin, 56 Situation Reports, 50 Special Focus, 53 Annual Review. Date value 8 means before 2020.
- Early items are a stub page plus a PDF.
- Sample: `/content/protection-civilians-weekly-report-1-7-oct-2003` → `/sites/default/files/WBN_07OCT.pdf`, 4 pages, 14,265 chars. It gives casualties, curfews by village with number of days, and demolitions by date and place.
- UNISPAL appears to hold the same briefing notes as text (seen in a search result, not verified).
- Humanitarian Monitor PDFs were seen only in search results.

**10. WAFA Info Centre** (Arabic).
- Pages are `https://info.wafa.ps/pages/details/N`; categories are `https://info.wafa.ps/Categorys/Details/N?pageNumber=k`. The sitemap is by modification day, 2022 onward.
- «سجل الخالدين» (category 206) lists martyrs of 1948, Kafr Qasim 1956, Karama 1968, Land Day 1976, Sabra and Shatila 1982, the first intifada, Al-Aqsa 1990, the Ibrahimi Mosque 1994, 1996, those assassinated 1967–2014, and the West Bank and Gaza by period.
- Sample: page 30750 «شهداء الضفة الغربية، 1990- 1999» → `https://info.wafa.ps/userfiles/server/pdf/west_bank_1990-1999.pdf`, 67 pages, 1,156 numbered rows with date of death and date of birth.
- `pdftotext` returns the dates but 0 Arabic characters, so the names need OCR or another extractor.

**11. LAW via Wayback.**
- Listing: `http://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=lawsociety.org&matchType=domain&collapse=urlkey&filter=statuscode:200&filter=mimetype:text/html&fl=timestamp,original`.
- Pages: `http://web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>id_/http://www.lawsociety.org/Press/Preleases/YYYY/Month/MonDD.html`.
- Sample: "LAW Press Release 29 July 2001 – Another attack on Al Aqsa", 1,499 chars.
- phrmg.org has at least 1,150 pages first captured 1999–2008 before spam took over the domain; the list was capped at 6,000, so this is partial.
- palestinercs.org has only about 30 early captures in that cap.

**12. FMEP.**
- Listing: `http://fmep.org/resource-sitemap.xml` and `resource-sitemap2.xml` (not fetched).
- Item: `https://fmep.org/resource/report-on-israeli-settlement-in-the-occupied-territories-vol-2-1/` → `https://fmep.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/2.1.pdf`.
- Sample: Vol 2 No 1, January 1992, 7 pages, 40,757 chars from the OCR text layer.

**13. IMEMC.**
- Listing: `https://imemc.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?per_page=100&page=N&order=asc&orderby=date&before=2016-01-01T00:00:00`.
- Sample: id 10173, 2003-05-29, "The Road Map: The Risk is as High as the Potential Created", 3,387 chars.
- At the stated 20 s delay the whole listing takes about two hours.

**14. PLO Research Center** (`prc.ps`, Arabic).
- Listing: `https://www.prc.ps/category/إصدارات-المركز/مجلدات-اليوميات-الفلسطينية?page=1..3`.
- Seen: vol. 1 = 1 Jan–30 Jun 1965; vols 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9 (to mid-1969); vols 22–24 = Jun 1975–Dec 1976; vols 25–33 = Oct 2018–Feb 2024. Page 2 was not fetched.
- The item page for vol. 8 (1 Jul–31 Dec 1968) describes a day-by-day record and links to a Google Drive file, which I did not fetch. Whether it is a scan or text is not verified.
- Recent volumes are PDFs on the site.
- Also there: «شؤون فلسطينية» at `/category/magazines?page=1..13` (page 1 shows issues 273–294; older pages not fetched), a monthly chronicle, and PNC and PLO documents.

**15. MachsomWatch.**
- Index: `https://www.machsomwatch.org/wp-json/wp/v2/content_daily_report?per_page=100&page=N&order=asc&orderby=date&before=2016-01-01T00:00:00`. It returns id, date, link and checkpoint tags but no body.
- The text is on `/content_daily_report/<slug>/`. There are also 58 `content_daily_report-sitemapN.xml` files and 939 military-court reports.
- Sample: "Morning", Sep-30-2002, about 1,100 chars of observation.
- The three oldest entries carry placeholder dates.

**16. Akevot.**
- Listing: `https://www.akevot.org.il/article-sitemap.xml` (261 URLs, 124 English). The JSON listing answers 401.
- Sample: "The Comay-Meron Cable reveals reasons for Israeli position on applicability of 4th Geneva Convention", 16,663 chars of page text including menus, plus `…/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Comay-MeronCable-Eng.pdf`. I did not extract the document's date.

**17. Hansard.**
- Listing: `https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/sittings/YYYY/mon/DD`.
- Sample: `/historic-hansard/commons/1948/may/14/palestine-military-situation`, HC Deb 14 May 1948 vol 450 cc2419-44, 55,667 chars.

**18. Al-Haq.**
- Lists: `https://www.alhaq.org/publications?page=1..17` and `/monitoring-documentation/{fieldwork-reports,monthly-reports,annual-reports,FromtheField}`.
- Items: `/publications/NNNN.html` with a PDF under `/cached_uploads/download/alhaq_files/publications/`. The sitemap answers 500.
- Sample: `/publications/8173.html`, "Human rights violations in the West Bank: in their own words" (a collection of affidavits), about 600 chars of description. The PDF was not downloaded, so scan versus text is not verified.
- The same page lists "Report on Human Rights Practices in the Occupied Territories During 1981".

**19. Palestine-Israel Journal.**
- `https://pij.org/sitemap.xml`; articles are `/articles/<id>/<slug>`. Only 2 slugs contain "chronology".
- Sample: `/articles/1212/…`, "Chronology of Events: July 1, 2008 to October 1, 2008", 15,442 chars.

**20. Zochrot.**
- `https://www.zochrot.org/sitemap.xml`; village pages are `/villages/village_details/<id>/<lang>?<Name>`.
- Sample: testimony 56367, "Ahmed Eissa Ibriq", 01/01/2007, refugee from Kuwaykat. It is metadata only, about 300 chars; the testimony itself is video.

**21. Palestine Remembered.**
- Pages: `https://www.palestineremembered.com/<District>/<Village>/index.html`.
- Sample: Qaqun (Tulkarm), 53,218 chars: occupied 5 June 1948, land ownership (Arab 35,611 dunums, Jewish 4,642), land use 1945, population 1596–1948, houses.

**22. Eyewitness sites** (all with the same `/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?...` listing).
- ISM, `palsolidarity.org`: from 2001-04-18. Sample: id 126, "Bidya roadblock removal", 1,912 chars.
- CPT, `cpt.org`: all regions. Sample: "Hebron Update: November 19-25, 2001", 4,296 chars, a day-by-day diary.
- `israelpalestinetimeline.org`: one post per person killed. Sample: "Sawsan Ibrahim Al-Kilani", July 21, 2014, 983 chars. Its date field is unreliable (years 0214 and 2011 on 2014 deaths).

**23. Ma'an.**
- `https://www.maannews.net/news/N.html`. ID 1000 = 04/06/2005; ID 100000 = 25/03/2008 («فياض يلتقي باراك غداً…», page text 4,342 chars); newest ID seen 2,170,452.
- Each page has previous/next links. There is no sitemap.

**24. WAFA: how far back.**
- English: `https://english.wafa.ps/Pages/Details/N`. ID 110000 = 5 Feb 2011 ("Israeli Security Arrests Former Prisoner from Jerusalem", about 915 chars); ID 117000 = 7 Apr 2013. The lowest live ID was not found.
- Arabic: `https://www.wafa.ps/sitemap.xml?yyyy=&mm=&dd=` starts 5 Feb 2020, and IDs restarted (ID 100000 is from 2024). The Arabic archive is therefore outside the window.

**25. Jrayed.**
- `https://jrayed.org/en/newspapers/titles`; year page `https://jrayed.org/en/newspapers/alittihad/1976/`.
- Runs past 1948: Al-Ittihad (Haifa, daily) 1944-05-14 → 2017-10-31; Al-Difa' to 1948-12-29; Filastin to 1948-04-24; Al-Baath (Tulkarem) 1948–51; Al-Sarih 1947–51.
- The landing page says the archive is "searchable by title and author name", which suggests page images without article text.
- No issue-level sample: one issue URL answered 404 and the month page 520.
- Terms: "solely for the non-commercial purposes of research, education and self-study".

**26. Low priority.**
- MIFTAH: `https://www.miftah.org/Display.cfm?DocId=N&CategoryId=C`, ids to about 29,898; DocId 5000 = 29 Sept 2004. Many are reposts.
- Palestine Chronicle: 12,331 posts before 2016, from Sept 2006, commentary.
- ARIJ: 720 posts before 2016, stubs with an embedded PDF viewer.
- Peace Now: 246 posts before 2016, Hebrew.
- Al-Zaytouna: 1,290 posts before 2016, from 2004.

- PCBS: `/en/publications/` is a script-paged table of statistical books (serials to 2734). curl fails on its TLS chain, so I read it through the reader proxy.

**27. No.**
- Al-Ayyam: print PDFs for 2005–2026 at `https://www.al-ayyam.ps/public/pdfs/YYYY/MM/DD/pNN.pdf`, but robots says `Disallow: /public/` for everyone except Googlebot, MSNBot and Facebook.
- Al-Quds: the site holds posts from April 2023 only. Its robots file names GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Anthropic-AI and others with `Allow: /` and crawl-delay 2.
- Al-Hayat al-Jadida: archive depth not established.
- WRMEA: robots says `Disallow: /` for ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Claude-Web, GPTBot, CCBot.
- ReliefWeb: the API host says `Disallow: /` and the site disallows paging; I did not call it.
- UN Digital Library: `/search` is disallowed, and the UN Yearbook is served through it.
- UN ODS (`documents.un.org`): `/doc` and `/api` are disallowed.
- Nakba Archive: video only.
