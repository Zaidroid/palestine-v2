## What the 12-row read found

The broad cue lists over-trigger: a sentence that contains a family word anywhere and a number anywhere is kept, even when the number belongs to another subject. The clean families are those whose cue is a **phrase** (trees, hunger strike, land orders, aid trucks, road gates); the noisy ones are single words (فقر, نسبة, سرير, صاروخ, دونم, شهيد). The run-on headline bodies (a whole bulletin pasted as one paragraph) are the second cause: the number and the cue can be in different headlines.
