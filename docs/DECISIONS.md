- 2026-08-01 · bug (LIVE INVERSION) · The confidence formula was `(1 - 0.15^agree) * trust_weight(LATEST reporter)`, which called itself a noisy-OR and was not one: it counted every unit as equally reliable, then scaled the total by whoever reported most recently.
