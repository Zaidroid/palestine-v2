### LEARNING-03 · F110 · medium · accuracy
**Loop A can never record a miss in a two-unit bucket and scores each unit against a consensus that includes its own vote**

- **Where:** `learn/reliability.py:175`
- **What goes wrong:** checkpoint_flow buckets are mostly copyset + one other unit (HANDOFF §6: 9 channels = 1 observer, a7walstreet independent). Two units agreeing -> both hit; two units disagreeing -> tie -> discarded. Misses exist only in >=3-unit buckets, so hits/(hits+misses) and the kappa computed over `joint` (scored buckets only) are inflated; HANDOFF §6 'all 15 reporters equally good, kappa 0.90-1.00' is partly this artifact. A crowd account that mirrors the channels accumulates hits and never a miss, reaches trust_weight ~1.0, and its solo caution is then served at 0.85 (finding 1 without the floor). The tie share is measurable only on main-server.
- **Fix:** Leave-one-out: for each unit compute the majority over the OTHER units in the bucket (with two units the other unit is the consensus, so a disagreement is a miss for both and an agreement a hit); require >=1 other unit; report ties and the share of two-unit buckets in the run output; add pure-Python tests on score_kind with synthetic rows (2 units disagreeing must yield one miss each).
